Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

What we collect

WordShift may collect information needed to provide accounts, leaderboards, races, and game features. This can include a username, account email address, gameplay statistics, leaderboard information, and technical information necessary to operate and secure the service.

Passwords

WordShift does not use or view your password as plain text. Authentication is handled by the service used to manage accounts. We do not intentionally sell, publish, or disclose your password.

Email addresses

Your email address may be used for account authentication, account recovery, and essential service-related communication. We do not sell your email address or use it for unrelated advertising.

How information is used

Information is used to operate WordShift, maintain accounts, sync progress, display leaderboards, enable races, prevent abuse, troubleshoot problems, and improve the service.

Third-party services

WordShift relies on third-party services to host data, provide authentication, and deliver parts of the website. Those providers process information as necessary to provide their services and have their own policies.

Data retention and deletion

Information may be retained while it is needed to provide the service. You may contact the site owner to ask questions about your information or request deletion where applicable.

Your choices

You can choose not to create an account and still use public parts of WordShift where available. Account features require the information needed to operate those features.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the WordShift owner through the contact method associated with the website.
